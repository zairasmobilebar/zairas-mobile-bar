FAQ

Everything to know
before the celebration.

Does Zaira's provide the alcohol?+

No. Zaira's operates as a dry-hire bartending service. The client/host purchases and provides all alcohol for the event. Zaira's does not sell, purchase, transport or store the client's alcohol.

What does Zaira's provide?+

Depending on the agreed package: bartending service, the transportable bar, bar tools, mixers, garnishes, cups, straws, napkins, menu planning, shopping-list guidance, setup and bar-area cleanup. The client provides alcohol and event ice.

Can you help us know how much alcohol to buy?+

Yes. Zaira's can provide shopping-list guidance based on guest count, service time and the chosen menu. The host makes the final alcohol purchase.

Is a second bartender available?+

Yes. A second bartender can be scheduled for larger guest counts or cocktail-heavy service.

Do you serve mocktails?+

Yes. Mocktails can be included alongside alcoholic drinks or planned as a fully alcohol-free event menu.

Do you offer bilingual service?+

Yes. Zaira is fluent in English and Spanish.

What area do you serve?+

Zaira's serves the Vancouver, Washington area and accepts Portland-area inquiries. Travel pricing may apply depending on the venue location.

What about Washington or Oregon alcohol requirements?+

Requirements vary by state, venue and event. Washington and Oregon have different alcohol-service rules, so any required venue approvals, permits or state-specific service requirements should be confirmed before the event.

How do I reserve a date?+

The current recommended terms are a signed service agreement plus a 25% non-refundable booking retainer, with the remaining balance due 14 days before the event. Final signed terms control.
